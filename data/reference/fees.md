# Fee and conditions reference

Compiled on 2026-10-15. Every figure carries the URL of the page or PDF that was opened. Anything seen only in a search summary is marked **not verified**. Amounts are gross euros unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Municipal Musikschule fees: AV MuS-Honorare

Source: [AV MuS-Honorare of 1.8.2022, with the table "Honorarsätze Musikschule 2026"](https://www.berlin.de/sen/kultur/kultureinrichtungen/musikschulen/av-mus-honorare-gesamt_neu.pdf) (18-page PDF, opened and read).

### 2026 table (in force since 1.1.2026, +5.50 %), per 45 minutes

| Honorarsatz | Activity | With full training or equivalent (a) | Without it (b) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Instrument and voice, individual or group; competition preparation; complementary subjects; courses, workshops, projects | **34.85** | 32.20 |
| 2 | Musikalische Grundbildung; **direction of ensembles, bands, orchestras and choirs**; studienvorbereitende Ausbildung | **38.18** | 35.54 |

Development of Honorarsatz 1 (a/b) in the same PDF: 2023 (+2.80 %) 31.53 / 29.13; 2025 (+4.76 %) 33.03 / 30.52; 2026 (+5.50 %) 34.85 / 32.20. Honorarsatz 2: 34.55 / 32.15; 36.19 / 33.68; 38.18 / 35.54. Honorarsatz 3: 37.59 / 34.90; 39.38 / 36.56; 41.54 / 38.58.

Relevant rules in the text:

- The fee includes preparation and follow-up work (§3(4) and §5(4) of the model contract). For Honorarsatz 6 at most eight units a day are paid.
- Rates follow the pay development of Land employees and are adjusted every 1 January (§3(5)); the Senate administration informs.
- Written framework contract plus individual assignments in text form; there is no right to be given assignments (§2(3) of the model). No service, no fee, except Ausfallhonorar.
- Ausfallhonorar: 100 % for up to 4 weeks and 75 % for another 6 weeks if the impediment is not the teacher's. Payment on the 15th of the following month if the invoice is submitted before the 5th.
- An extended police clearance certificate (erweitertes Führungszeugnis, not older than three months and renewed every five years) is required.
- The school provides rooms and instruments free of charge "im Rahmen ihrer Möglichkeiten"; no lessons in school holidays or on public holidays.
- For "arbeitnehmerähnliche Personen" (§7): holiday under BUrlG, Bildungszeit, and Ausfallhonorar for illness of 95 % from the fourth day for at most six weeks.
- On taxes and social security: the Land does not withhold taxes; the teacher must register on their own with the Künstlersozialversicherung if they meet the requirements (§4 and model).

### What replaces the AV in 2027

- **Expiry:** §8 says the AV "tritt mit Ablauf des 31. Dezember 2026 außer Kraft".
- **No successor AV or new 2027 table was found.** The Senate administration page for Musikschulen (https://www.berlin.de/sen/kultur/kultureinrichtungen/musikschulen/) says nothing about 2027; the latest date is the 2022 AV. The Honorar conditions for 2027 are unknown.
- **Verified context:**
  - The Senate reserved 4 million euros for 2026 to move Honorarkräfte into permanent posts; the allocation of "up to 4 million" is made in 2026 and 2027 according to each district's lesson volume ([Drucksache 19/26581, answer of 17.7.2026](https://pardok.parlament-berlin.de/starweb/adis/citat/VT/19/SchrAnfr/S19-26581.pdf)).
  - By 30.6.2026 only about 33,100 euros had been spent; many districts are waiting for the new school year. Explicit data by district: Neukölln takes on 10 people on 1.9.2026 and another 10 during 2026; Treptow-Köpenick, 3.75 full-time equivalents on 1.9.2026.
  - All districts ask for application documents; most hold an interview and several a Lehrprobe or Vorspiel. In Treptow-Köpenick the Lehrprobe takes place "bei Bedarf, sofern sich Bewerbende von außerhalb durchsetzen; bislang war dies nicht erforderlich", that is, so far no external candidate has prevailed.
  - According to ver.di (25.2.2026), about three quarters of the roughly 2,400 teachers at the 12 Musikschulen work on Honorar, almost 2,000 are still treated as self-employed under the Übergangsregelung until the end of 2027, the coalition reserved 8 million for 2026/27 for about 200 posts, and ver.di estimates this covers only a quarter of the need ([ver.di](https://www.verdi.de/kunst-kultur/musik/herrenberg-urteil-folgen-fuer-honorarkraefte-und-musikschulen)).
  - The Übergangsregelung of §127 SGB IV was extended by one year, to 31.12.2027 (same source, article of 25.2.2026).
- **Reasonable forecast, not published:** because §3(5) ties the rates to the TV-L tables, the TV-L increase of 1.3.2027 (+2.0 % according to [oeffentlichen-dienst.de](https://oeffentlichen-dienst.de/entgelttabelle/tv-l.html)) indicates the order of magnitude of the next adjustment, but this is not a rule.

## 2. Permanent post: TV-L and the usual grade of a Musikschullehrkraft

Source: [Tarifvertrag zur Regelung der Eingruppierung der Musikschullehrkräfte des Landes Berlin (TV Musikschullehrkräfte Land Berlin), 3.5.2016](https://www.berlin.de/politik-und-verwaltung/rundschreiben/download.php/4323883) (PDF opened and read). It applies to Musikschullehrkräfte at Musikschulen of the Land Berlin that fall under the TV-L.

- **Entgeltgruppe 9**: Musikschullehrkräfte with "berufsfachliche Ausbildung" and a corresponding activity. The "berufsfachliche Ausbildung" includes the Master of Music or Master of Arts with an artistic profile (at least eight semesters) that builds on an artistic Bachelor, and the Bachelor (at least six semesters), all at a state or recognised Hochschule. For foreign degrees, recognition is assessed by the district.
- **Without that training** (Protokollerklärung 1(3)): one group lower.
- **Entgeltgruppe 10**: a person who, with a written order, gives an average of **8 teaching hours of 45 min per week** in studienvorbereitende Ausbildung, or directs ensembles/orchestras/choirs, or does Früherziehung/Grundausbildung in cooperation with a Kita or school, or teaches groups of at least four pupils, or teaches two subjects with at least 25 % each, or does Korrepetition. Hours add up if several of these activities occur (pro rata for part-time).
- **EG 10 and 11**: direction of a Fachgruppe (by volume of hours or pupils); **EG 11** also direction of a Zweigstelle; EG 12 to 15: direction or deputy direction of the Musikschule.
- A Funktionszulage of 100 euros a month for directing a Fachgruppe with at least 150 annual weekly lesson hours (Protokollerklärung 2, only together with EG 9).

## 3. Private schools: commission and room rent

**No published data for Berlin.** None of the private schools that were opened publishes a commission on pupils or a room rent:

- [Musikschule Ungefucht](https://www.musikschule-ungefucht.de/berlin/instrumentallehrer-gesucht): lessons "auf Honorarbasis"; they may be given in the teacher's own rooms; it receives pupil enquiries regularly; no rates or rent are quoted.
- [WunderMusikSchule](https://wundermusikschule.de/mehr/stellenangebote/): "ca. 40 Lehrkräfte, die selbstständig auf Honorarbasis arbeiten"; "überdurchschnittlich hohe Honorare" without figures.

**Market references** (recommendations, not school prices): [Deutscher Musikrat, Honoraruntergrenze und angemessene Vergütungen im Bereich der Musikalischen Bildung, update of 1.3.2026](https://www.musikrat.de/fileadmin/user_upload/2026_02_25_DMR_Positionspapier_HUG_im_Bereich_Musikalische_Bildung.pdf): minimum 56 euros per 45 min and 74 euros per 60 min; adequate pay 67 euros per 45 min and 89 euros per 60 min; covers preparation, own room, insurance, training and retirement provision.

**Legal framework for choosing Honorar or a direct contract:**

- The [Arbeitsgericht Berlin, judgment of 15.7.2025, Az. 22 Ca 10650/24](https://www.berlin.de/gerichte/arbeitsgericht/presse/pressemitteilungen/2025/pressemitteilung.1583439.php) (press release read) held that a Musikschule teacher with a freie Mitarbeit contract since 1999 was not an employee: she chose place, time and content and could refuse pupils without reason. The Deutsche Rentenversicherung had decided in June 2024 that she was an employee for social-security purposes, a decision that was not final; the court said it does not bind in labour law. An appeal is possible.
- Providing services with the school's rooms, schedules and requirements comes close to an employment relationship. The more freedom (pupils, place, time), the sounder the self-employment.

## 4. Social security for self-employed teachers

### Rentenversicherung

- **Obligation:** self-employed "Lehrer und Erzieher" who "regelmäßig keinen versicherungspflichtigen Arbeitnehmer beschäftigen" must contribute to the public pension scheme; people in marginal employment (geringfügig) do not count as employees ([§2 SGB VI](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/sgb_6/__2.html), read). "Künstler und Publizisten" under the KSVG are a different case (next section).
- **Rate:** 18.6 % in total in 2026 (the KSK charges the insured only half, 9.3 %); the calculation base for self-employed people was not verified (ask the DRV).
- **Herrenberg:** the DRV states that since 1.3.2025 there has been a transition rule for teachers and clients; self-employed people can apply for a Statusfeststellungsverfahren; if the service is dependent there is compulsory insurance with half paid by the employer ([DRV, Herrenberg-Urteil](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Home/Herrenberg-Urteil/herrenberg-node)). The extension to 31.12.2027 is in the ver.di article cited above.

### Künstlersozialkasse (KSK)

Source: [KSK, Voraussetzungen](https://www.kuenstlersozialkasse.de/kuenstler-und-publizisten/voraussetzungen) and [Beitrag](https://www.kuenstlersozialkasse.de/kuenstler-und-publizisten/beitrag) (read).

- **Who:** "Künstler ist, wer Musik, darstellende oder bildende Kunst schafft, ausübt oder lehrt"; self-employed, professional and not merely temporary activity. Teaching music therefore falls within the legal definition; the KSK decides each case.
- **Minimum:** income above **3,900 euros a year (325 a month)**; in the first three years of activity it applies even if this is not reached.
- **Exception:** if more than one insured employee is employed (other than trainees or marginal), there is no KSK.
- **Insured person's contributions in 2026 (roughly half of the total):** pension 9.3 % (total 18.6 %); health 7.3 % plus half of the Zusatzbeitrag (total 14.6 %); long-term care 1.8 % with children or 2.4 % without (totals 3.6 % and 4.2 %).
- **With Honorar and no KSK**, the teacher pays the full contributions (the totals above). For comparison with a direct contract: as an employee, the worker pays about half of those totals (plus unemployment insurance, not verified here).

### Minijob and minimum wage

[Press release DRV Baden-Württemberg](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/BadenWuerttemberg/DE/Presse/Pressemitteilungen/2025/251222_Minijob.html): Minijob limit **603 euros a month in 2026** and **633 euros in 2027**; minimum wage **13.90 euros per hour in 2026** and **14.60 euros planned for 2027**.

## 5. Other pay references used by the categories

- **Lehrauftrag at Berlin Hochschulen:** the FU guideline, based on the Senate administration's Ausführungsvorschriften of 21.9.2023 (§120(5) BerlHG), sets a minimum per teaching hour (at least 45 min) of 45.23 euros (winter semester 2025/26), **46.81 euros (2026/27)** and 48.45 euros (2027/28), with a maximum of double in justified cases ([Amtsblatt FU 41/2023](https://www.fu-berlin.de/service/zuvdocs/amtsblatt/2023/ab412023.pdf)). Other Hochschulen may have their own systems without going below that minimum (according to a search summary of the Senate answer, not verified).
- **Funded projects:** [Senatsverwaltung für Kultur, Honoraruntergrenzen, April 2026](https://www.berlin.de/sen/kultur/foerderung/foerderprogramme/downloads/honoraruntergrenzen_stand-april-2026.pdf): music (Neue Musik, Alte Musik, Jazz), **375 euros per day with KSK** as a floor; the "Berliner Modell" of DACH Musik (October 2024) aims at 736 euros per day, starting at 60 % (442 euros).
- **Deutscher Musikrat, working day of a self-employed musician in federally funded projects:** 300 euros for 2025/26; "auskömmlich" floor 622 euros ([DMR note](https://www.musikrat.de/en/media/latest-news/meldung/der-deutsche-musikrat-gibt-empfehlungen-fuer-honoraruntergrenzen-bei-bkm-foerderung)).
- **Music outreach (Musikvermittlung):** [Barenboim-Said Akademie, "Musik spricht"](https://www.barenboimsaid.de/Administration/HR/2026/BSA_Ausschreibung_Mentor_innen_Musikspricht.pdf): Honorarvertrag, **40 euros per 60 min**, about 10 hours a month, from 1.10.2026 (deadline 23.8.2026, expired).
